# DRAWING_INSTRUCTIONS.md

## Purpose

This file stores the user's long-term, commonly used drawing prompt and visual preferences. It is a shared baseline for all ACCOUNTs in the `lora_20` project.

## Clothing Color Preference

- Main clothing color: water blue (水藍色)
- Secondary clothing color: navy blue (藏青色)

## Flower Preference

- Frequently used flower varieties: Japanese blue starflower (日本藍星花) / blue butterfly pea flower (藍色粉蝶花)
- For each scene, the primary flower variety should be **one variety only**.
- The scene may include other flower varieties when appropriate to the setting.
- The two frequently used varieties do not need to appear together in every image.

## Image Quality and Visual Style

- Overall atmosphere: romantic, luxurious/high-end, beautiful, dreamy, Japanese airy aesthetic (日系空氣感)
- Lighting and skin: delicate light and shadow; naturally luminous skin
- Quality: best quality, ultra-clear, ultra-high resolution
- Resolution preference: 8K

## Composition

- Vertical composition: 9:16
- Horizontal composition: 16:9
- Use one composition ratio per image according to the current task; do not apply both ratios to the same image.

## Key Anatomy and Pose Requirements

### Hands

- Exactly five fingers on each hand.
- Finger joints and proportions must conform to realistic human anatomy.
- Palm and wrist must connect naturally.
- No fused, missing, duplicated, or malformed fingers.

### Feet

- Exactly five toes on each foot.
- Foot proportions must conform to realistic human anatomy.
- Shoes must not contain abnormal toes.
- When barefoot, toe arrangement must be natural.

### Pose

- Each generated image should use a new standing pose or action and avoid repeating existing compositions in the dataset.
- Body center of gravity, limb angles, and joint transitions must follow human ergonomics.

## Negative Prompt

### Chinese

低畫質、模糊、過度AI感、過度磨皮、五官變形、手部異常、腳部異常、多餘肢體、肢體畸形、誇張妝容、塑膠皮膚、低俗色情化表達、文字、水印、Logo、邊框。

### English

worst quality, low quality, lowres, blurry, jpeg artifacts, deformed, bad anatomy, bad hands, bad fingers, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, mutated hands, malformed hands, broken hands, extra limbs, missing limbs, bad feet, malformed feet, extra toes, missing toes, twisted toes, distorted body, duplicate body, duplicate limbs, incorrect anatomy, unnatural pose, cropped, watermark, text, logo, out of frame, disfigured face, crossed eyes, doll hands, AI hands, mutated limbs

## Cross-Account Rule

All ACCOUNTs must use this file as a shared baseline. An ACCOUNT must not independently replace these long-term preferences with its own preferred art style.

Historical images, previous chats, or previous requests for unrelated art styles must not override this baseline.
