# SPECIAL FESTIVAL WALLPAPER — VIEW RULES

## Purpose

Special-festival wallpapers are designed to present the character together with festival-specific clothing, accessories, hairstyle, makeup, body decoration, props, and cultural visual elements.

The primary goal is clear and attractive **front-facing presentation of the festival styling**, not viewpoint diversity for its own sake.

## VIEW POLICY

### Allowed
- FRONT
- FRONT_3/4_LEFT
- FRONT_3/4_RIGHT
- SIDE_LEFT
- SIDE_RIGHT

### Prohibited
- BACK
- BACK_3/4

## RULE

**Special-festival wallpapers MUST NOT use a back-facing character view.**

Do not design or request a composition in which the character's back is the primary visible character surface.

The character should remain sufficiently front-facing that the following can be presented clearly whenever relevant:
- festival clothing
- clothing construction and major decorative elements
- accessories
- headwear
- hairstyle
- makeup / face decoration
- body decoration
- festival props
- overall character appearance

## FRONT / 3/4 PRIORITY

FRONT and FRONT_3/4 are the preferred viewpoints.

SIDE views are allowed as supporting viewpoints when they improve:
- garment silhouette
- fabric drape
- layered clothing
- hairstyle profile
- accessory visibility
- overall composition

Do not force SIDE views when they hide important festival elements.

## IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

This rule applies **only to the Special Festival Wallpaper system**.

- LoRA dataset: BACK and BACK_3/4 remain allowed when useful for dataset viewpoint diversity.
- General wallpaper system: BACK and BACK_3/4 remain allowed.
- Special Festival Wallpaper system: BACK and BACK_3/4 are prohibited.

Do not copy the Special Festival Wallpaper restriction into the LoRA or General Wallpaper rules.

## PRIORITY

For Special Festival Wallpaper:

1. Character reference / identity
2. GitHub festival and cultural data
3. Cultural correctness
4. Clear festival recognition
5. Clear presentation of festival clothing and accessories
6. Stable anatomy and natural pose
7. Natural action
8. Scene and atmosphere
9. Wallpaper composition
10. Visual diversity and creativity

A viewpoint must not be selected if it substantially hides the festival styling that the image is intended to showcase.
