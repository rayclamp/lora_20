## Classification and navigation (P0-09)

The table above remains the **single canonical F001–F048 ID/name lookup**. All 48 existing IDs, English names, Chinese names, and their order are preserved; classification is **supplementary navigation metadata**, not a rename, historical migration, or modification to any festival-specific record.

### Type semantics

- `CORE_OBSERVANCE`: an explicitly indexed celebration, observance, or commemorative event. **Core** means an index entry, not proof it is entirely unrelated to other observances.
- `REGIONAL_VARIANT`: a specific geographic/cultural presentation or regional focus. This does **not** mean one culture's tradition originates from or is identical to another.
- `CROSS_CULTURAL_COLLECTION`: a broad umbrella / comparative topic containing multiple distinct practices. Use region-specific records rather than merging their ritual details.
- `SEASON_OR_PERIOD`: an observance period, cultural season, or seasonal activity; it need not denote a single holiday or single-day event.
- `THEMATIC_FOCUS`: a special visual/topic focus under an existing observance, **not** a newly invented independent holiday; specifically F038 remains a Qingming memorial focus related to F020.

A row has one **primary class** for browsing and may have multiple **culture / region tags**. Those tags indicate relevant navigation contexts, not a claim of origin, equivalence, exclusive ownership, universally shared ceremonies, or independently verified cultural facts. Related IDs denote **lookup / comparison / broader context** only; a related entry is never automatically interchangeable. Parenthetical notes next to related IDs explain important distinctions.

- The index is authoritative for **registered festival ID, existing names, and current lookup/classification**, but registration or a tag **does not prove that a costume, ritual, prop, region attribution, or cultural claim has independent source verification**.
- `FESTIVAL_CULTURAL_ANCHORS.md` supplies working design cues, explicitly **not** a source-verified cultural reference on its own; consult source-backed festival/item-level records for culturally specific statements, especially HIGH / VERY HIGH sensitivity contexts.
- For a selected F-ID, read the corresponding festival data, applicable tags, relevant item records, and their available provenance before deciding which visual details are culturally supported. Preserve uncertainty and distinguish a design cue from a verified tradition.
- Keep F001–F048 all intact: no deletion, renumbering, consolidation, or silent conversion of overlapping entries. F038 is deliberately retained as a distinct **index entry** even though it is not a separate holiday from F020.
