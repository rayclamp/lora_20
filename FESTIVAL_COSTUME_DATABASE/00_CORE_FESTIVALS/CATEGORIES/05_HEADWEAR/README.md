# HEADWEAR

Festival hats, head coverings, and culturally meaningful headwear.

## Catalog

Current candidates and their current statuses are listed in `ITEM_CATALOG.md`. This README defines category scope, not the item inventory. A full item record is required before any catalog item can be culturally VERIFIED.

## Complete source-reviewed item records

- `ITEMS/HEAD_EASTER_BONNET_001.md` — verified **only** for United States Easter Parade / New York City bonnet usage, supported by museum and Library of Congress references. The catalog's original unsourced broad geography was narrowed for this item; F006 `CATEGORY_LINKS` and `FESTIVAL_ITEM_LINK_MATRIX.md` now both include HEADWEAR.
- `ITEMS/HEAD_MIDSUMMER_FLOWER_001.md` — source-verified **F046 Swedish Midsummer flower crown**, supported by Visit Sweden; does not certify other Nordic regions or `HEAD_MID_AU_FLOWER_002`.
- All other active HEADWEAR catalog rows remain `UNVERIFIED`. The F008 `HEAD_STPATRICK_FLATCAP_001` was historically preserved and retired from the active catalog at P0-38; its former category-scope mismatch is resolved by retirement, not by cultural verification. A complete item record does not validate unrelated costumes or mandatory religious practice.
