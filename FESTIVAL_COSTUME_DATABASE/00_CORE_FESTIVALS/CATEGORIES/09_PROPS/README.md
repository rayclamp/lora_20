# PROPS

Festival objects, handheld items, and scene-compatible cultural props.

## Catalog

Current items and their actual `VERIFICATION_STATUS` are listed in `ITEM_CATALOG.md`. This README defines category scope, not the full inventory, and most original rows remain unverified.

## Complete source-reviewed items

- `ITEMS/PROP_US_THANKSGIVING_ROAST_TURKEY_001.md`: verified F007 roast turkey as a **modern United States Thanksgiving holiday dinner food prop**, documented by Smithsonian and Library of Congress sources. This is not evidence that all Canadian Thanksgiving traditions are identical, that every household serves turkey, or that a roast turkey centerpiece at the 1621 Plymouth gathering is definitively established.
- `ITEMS/PROP_GION_FLOAT_001.md`: source-verified **Kyoto F024 Gion Matsuri Yamahoko** as a **large procession / festival street scene element**, not a portable hand-held item. Kyoto City Official Guide and Japan National Tourism Organization directly document its festival significance. F024 already includes `PROPS`; no category or matrix edit is needed.
- `ITEMS/PROP_TANZAKU_001.md`: source-verified **Japanese F023 Tanabata tanzaku wish strips**, used for writing wishes and hanging on bamboo; JNTO and Kyoto City Official Guide document this association. The source review does not prescribe one universal date, design or inscription.
- `ITEMS/PROP_HALLOWEEN_PUMPKIN_001.md`: source-verified **F002 carved jack-o'-lantern** for modern United States Halloween seasonal decor, grounded in Library of Congress and Smithsonian historical evidence. This does not prove identical customs in all regions or equate earlier European turnip lanterns with American pumpkins.
- The original F002, F007, F023 and F024 festival records all already include `PROPS` within their allowed `CATEGORY_LINKS`. No new festival category is authorized or needed; these four reviewed props' presence does not make any unrelated catalog entry verified.
