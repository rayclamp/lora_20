## SENSITIVITY SCALE — RISK FLAG, NOT VERIFICATION

Original values LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH and VERY HIGH are retained exactly for every F001–F048 row.

- LOW: relatively lower misrepresentation risk; does not imply verified cultural accuracy.
- MEDIUM: check regional and context-specific claims before identifying a practice as traditional.
- HIGH: exercise heightened care for culturally, religiously, historically, or ritually specific assertions; use applicable source-backed item records.
- VERY HIGH: heightened consequence of inaccurate representation of sacred, ritual, ancestral, or regional practices; verify the exact claim and regional/religious context before describing it as established tradition.

Sensitivity is a design caution indicator only; it is NOT an evidence grade, recognition score, historical verification, cultural importance ranking, authenticity certification, or QA PASS. A LOW label does not certify accuracy; a VERY HIGH label does not mean the festival is inherently wrong.
