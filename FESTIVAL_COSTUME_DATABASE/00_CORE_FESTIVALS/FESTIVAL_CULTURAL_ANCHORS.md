## USE

- These anchors define recognizable generation cues.
- They do not authorize mixing unrelated regional variants.
- For high/very-high sensitivity festivals, workers must keep region, religious context, and ritual elements coherent.
- Generic decorative elements must not be presented as culturally specific unless supported by the festival record.
