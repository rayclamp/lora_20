## RULE — SOURCE OF TRUTH AND MISMATCH

- For the registered festival's ID and English name, follow `CORE_FESTIVAL_INDEX.md`. For the selected festival's allowed category set, **that festival record's `CATEGORY_LINKS` is authoritative**. This matrix is only the navigation view, and cannot silently add, remove, or override a festival record's categories.
- If the matrix and festival record disagree on categories, stop **item selection for that affected festival**, report `CATEGORY_LINK_MISMATCH` with the exact festival ID and differing values, and reconcile the discrepancy through the separately authorized correction process before selection. Do not silently reinterpret or automatically update any festival file.
- A listed category authorizes discovery of candidate items, **not** the claim that any candidate is traditional, historically accurate, regionally appropriate, religiously valid, or verified. Check exact festival ID, region, cultural context, ritual restrictions, item-level source evidence, and the active verification status first.
- Prefer a documented, appropriately verified traditional option where the evidence supports it. Modern options can be supplemental, but visual appeal or a category link is not enough to manufacture traditional significance.

## RELATED FESTIVAL / VARIANT BOUNDARIES

These notes clarify ID interpretation **without changing any original category links**. The P0-09 `CORE_FESTIVAL_INDEX.md` classification and related-ID entries are navigation metadata, not source evidence or licenses to merge traditions.

- **F011 / F044:** F011 denotes Mexican Día de los Muertos; F044 denotes the separately indexed Andean / other Latin American regional variants. **Do not transfer Mexican F011 costume, altar, makeup or ritual items to F044** on visual similarity alone; a source-backed, region-appropriate item record is required for such claims.
- **F020 / F038:** F038 is a memorial/tomb-sweeping **thematic focus overlapping the same Qingming observance as F020**, not a newly independent holiday. Keep both registered IDs and their existing distinct category mappings, without inventing an unrelated second festival.
- **F018 / F028 / F033 / F039:** F039 is a cross-cultural East Asian Mid-Autumn collection/lookup; F018, F028 and F033 are regional entries. Do not assume region-specific attire, rituals, foods or festival items are interchangeable merely because they share a broad theme.
- **F021 / F035:** The Lantern Festival and Taiwan Lantern Festival are separately indexed entries. Related lantern imagery alone does not authorize exchanging ritual claims, historical provenance, or region-specific items.
