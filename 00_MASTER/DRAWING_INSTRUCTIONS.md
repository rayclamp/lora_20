# DRAWING_INSTRUCTIONS.md — Inaria Drawing Instructions

## Authority
Use 00_MASTER/ANATOMY_STABILITY.md as the complete mandatory anatomy and generation-stability specification.

## Visual direction
- Romantic, refined, beautiful, dreamy Japanese airy atmosphere.
- Delicate natural lighting and shadow.
- Natural luminous skin appearance; avoid plastic over-retouching.
- High quality, clean, detailed illustration.
- Water-blue and navy are common project colors, not mandatory identity colors.

## Composition
- 16:9 and 9:16 are valid task-defined formats.
- Front or side-front views are preferred unless the approved task explicitly requires another view.
- Keep hands away from image edges.
- Keep important background objects and effects away from hands and fingers.
- Avoid unnecessary effects around fingers and toes.

## Anatomy-first drawing
Before decorative complexity:
1. Confirm exactly two hands and two legs.
2. Confirm traceable shoulder/arm/wrist/palm and hip/leg/ankle/foot connections.
3. Design stable fingers/toes.
4. Confirm support, center of gravity, joint direction, and force/load.
5. Add clothing and accessories.
6. Add background, props, lighting effects, and decorative details.

Follow ANATOMY_STABILITY.md for single-hand actions, stable grips, hand-object contact, feet/lower-body stability, long-skirt sofa sitting, bags/straps, containers, false-limb prevention, and local repair.

## Local editing
When only a local region is requested, preserve unrelated identity, style, lighting, color, clothing, background, and composition whenever technically feasible.

## Negative baseline
worst quality, low quality, lowres, blurry, jpeg artifacts, deformed, bad anatomy, bad hands, bad fingers, extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, mutated hands, malformed hands, broken hands, extra limbs, missing limbs, bad feet, malformed feet, extra toes, missing toes, twisted toes, distorted body, duplicate body, duplicate limbs, incorrect anatomy, unnatural pose, cropped, watermark, text, logo, out of frame, disfigured face, crossed eyes, doll hands, AI hands, mutated limbs

## Historical style isolation
Past chats, other projects, or an account's personal generation style must never replace the current MASTER_IMAGE reference and 00_MASTER rules.
