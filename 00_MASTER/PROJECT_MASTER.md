# Inaria AI Studio — Project Master

## Purpose
This repository is the shared coordination space for six independent ChatGPT accounts working on Inaria-related character, clothing, scene, pose/camera, prompt, and quality-control tasks.

## Collaboration principle
- `00_MASTER/` is the source of truth for stable project rules.
- Specialist accounts should read the relevant MASTER files before working.
- Specialist outputs belong in their numbered workspace.
- Do not silently overwrite another specialist's work.
- Use versioned filenames (`v001`, `v002`, etc.).
- Approved decisions should be recorded in `06_DIRECTOR/` and, when they become permanent rules, promoted into `00_MASTER/` by the project owner/director.
