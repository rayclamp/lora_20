# Art Style Master

## Core visual direction
- Romantic, refined, dreamy Japanese-inspired atmosphere (日系空氣感).
- Delicate light and shadow.
- Natural, translucent-looking skin texture.
- High-quality, clean, detailed rendering.
- Avoid an overly artificial or plastic AI appearance.
- Preserve natural facial texture and believable anatomy.

## Color direction
- Water blue (水藍色) is a preferred primary accent.
- Navy (藏青色) is a preferred secondary accent.
- Exact palette may change per project; do not force these colors when the task specifies another palette.

## Quality exclusions
Avoid low quality, blur, excessive smoothing, deformed facial features, hand/foot anomalies, extra limbs, exaggerated makeup, plastic-looking skin, text, watermark, and logo unless a task explicitly requires an exception.

## Composition preference
- Current wallpaper work may use either horizontal 16:9 or vertical 9:16 depending on the active task.
- Keep important background objects from overlapping hands whenever possible.
- Avoid excessive visual effects around fingers and toes.
