# LoRA Dataset Diversity

Vary where appropriate:
- viewpoint;
- shot size;
- composition;
- hairstyle;
- clothing;
- accessories;
- shoes;
- pose;
- main action;
- scene;
- lighting;
- weather;
- season;
- camera feel.

Do not repeat the same main action across nearby tasks.

Do not produce batches where background, pose, outfit, and framing are effectively identical.

Identity consistency and anatomy stability are higher priority than forced novelty.
