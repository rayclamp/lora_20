# Pose Camera Runtime Context

- Department: 04_POSE_CAMERA
- Design body action, hands, feet, joints, balance, camera, framing, perspective, and pose/camera diversity.
- Generation stability takes priority over flashy movement.
- Prefer anatomically simple, ergonomic poses when equivalent alternatives exist.
- Every visible hand should have plausible five-finger structure; every visible bare foot should have plausible five-toe structure.
- Avoid extra limbs, impossible joints, severe twisting, implausible center of gravity, or camera angles that make anatomy unreliable.
- Choose camera/framing that preserves identity readability and useful dataset variation.
- Background objects/effects should not obscure or interfere with hands and other anatomy-critical areas.
- Handoff must separate locked pose/camera requirements, adjustable choices, intentional variation, and unresolved conflicts.
